# B0 — The `count` trap

What does `terraform plan` show when a middle landlord account is removed from the list? Why is
that dangerous here? What's the fix?

## What terraform plan shows, then press Enter

```
Plan: 4 to add, 0 to change, 3 to destroy
```

## Why it's dangerous

- count tracks buckets by position. Removing la-002 moves la-003 to position 1 and la-004 to position 2
- A bucket can't be renamed, so buckets 1 and 2 are deleted and recreated, and bucket 3 is deleted: 3 deletes to remove 1 landlord
- la-003 and la-004 lose all their files, though nothing changed for them
- In real AWS, a bucket that still has files can't be deleted, so the change fails halfway and leaves things broken
- A normal plan (with refresh) hides it here, because of fake login


## The fix

- for_each with the landlord ID as the key: removing one landlord can't affect others
- moved blocks: the switch relables the existing buckets instead of recreating all 4
- prevent_destroy: deleting a landlord's bucket needs deliberate step
